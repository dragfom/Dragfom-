About the product

About DRAGFOM

DRAGFOM Studio · Mobile-first 3D design workspace

DRAGFOM is a browser-based 3D design and editing workspace for creating, styling, animating and exporting 3D scenes. It is designed especially with mobile users in mind, aiming to make common 3D workflows more accessible on phones and tablets through a touch-oriented interface, while also supporting desktop browsers.

What Can You Create?

  • 3D visuals and interactive scenes for websites
  • 3D objects and showcase scenes for portfolio projects
  • Concepts and prototype assets for 3D games
  • Product-style presentations and object animations
  • Experiments with Three.js, WebGL materials and GLSL shader effects

Mobile-Friendly by Design

DRAGFOM is developed with small screens and touch interaction in mind. The goal is to help creators start working with 3D scenes directly in a modern mobile browser, without requiring a desktop-only workflow. Performance and feature availability may vary by device, browser and scene complexity.

Export Your Work

Use the available Download options to export supported work as HTML or Three.js JavaScript. Check exported files before publishing them and make sure you have permission to use every included asset.

Product Scope

DRAGFOM focuses on accessible browser-based 3D creation. It is not a full game engine, and the current launch version does not provide account-based cloud project storage. Available features may evolve as the product develops.
